Projects

Does recovery style affect boat speed?

A first-principles physics model of the rowing stroke, built from scratch in Python.

Finding

The looser, back-loaded recovery is faster by ~0.33 boat lengths over 700 m — not by staying near top speed longer, but because it never builds the forward surge that steep v³ drag punishes in the front-loaded style.
